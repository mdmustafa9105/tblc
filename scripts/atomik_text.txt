FAQs
What’s the typical turnaround time?
Seven days from recording to release. You record on day one, and by day seven your podcast episode, YouTube videos, social clips, and articles are ready to publish across your channels.
Do I need my own recording equipment?
For remote recordings, we recommend a quality USB microphone. For in-person sessions, we can arrange professional studio bookings in most major cities and provide full technical guidance during onboarding.
How much time do I need to commit each week?
Just one hour for recording. We handle pre-production, editing, distribution, social media, and reporting so your team can stay focused on the business.
What platforms do you distribute to?
We create and optimize content for Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, and your own website, tailoring the format and timing to each channel.
What makes Atomik different from other agencies?
We specialize in content and distribution for technology companies and venture capital firms. That focus means we understand the audience, language, and growth goals behind every piece of content.
