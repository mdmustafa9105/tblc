## 1. Executive Reference Overview

Atomik Growth is the premier benchmark for high-velocity, high-credibility new-media agencies. Operating primarily within the venture-backed technology, AI, and startup sectors, Atomik Growth has engineered a website experience characterized by:

1. **Immediate Category Definition:** An unapologetic, single-sentence category claim ("The new media arm for ambitious tech companies").
2. **Instant Visual Proof:** Immediate deployment of client traction and output before any service descriptions or sales persuasion.
3. **Living Output as UI:** Abandoning abstract icons and generic bullet points in favor of interactive smartphone mockups playing real, high-performing vertical short-form video.
4. **Granular Metric Verification:** Dedicated data displays featuring verified reach and platform-by-platform case study KPIs.
5. **Founder Authority Integration:** Demonstrating organic audience mastery by integrating founder-led media directly into the sales journey.
6. **Singular Conversion Architecture:** A relentless, non-distracting path to a 20-minute introductory calendar call.

### The Intellectual Property Boundary
The Book Launch Company will recreate the **visible structural architecture, spatial pacing, typographic hierarchy, component interactions, and conversion mechanics** of Atomik Growth without copying proprietary code, logos, branding, copy, or graphics. All patterns are translated into a bespoke, high-end literary identity.

### Section 00: Navigation Bar

#### 1. Atomik Growth Original Composition
* A compact, floating horizontal pill centered at the top of the viewport.
* Sits ~24px below the top browser edge, detached from the viewport borders.
* Contains brand wordmark on the far left, centered navigation anchors (`Services`, `Case Studies`, `Podcast`, `About`), and a high-contrast primary CTA button (`Book a Call`) on the far right.
* Background: Translucent dark surface with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle 1px border.

#### 2. Layout & Proportions
* Desktop width: ~960px to 1080px (never 100% full width at rest).
* Height: 56px to 64px.
* Border radius: Full pill (`rounded-full`, 9999px).
* Padding: 8px 16px 8px 24px.

#### 3. Typography & Spacing
* Links: Modern sans-serif, 14px (`text-sm`), medium weight (500), uppercase or clean title-case with subtle tracking (`tracking-wide`).
* Spacing between links: 24px to 32px.
* CTA button: 14px semibold with 8px vertical / 16px horizontal padding.

#### 4. Interaction & Motion Behavior
* Shrinks slightly and gains higher background opacity upon scrolling (`scroll > 50px`).
* Link hover: Subtle brightness shift (`opacity: 0.7 → 1.0`) with a smooth 200ms transition.
* CTA hover: Micro-scale feedback (`transform: translateY(-1px)` or subtle shadow glow).
* Mobile view: Condenses into a brand logo + hamburger toggle. Tapping opens a full-screen drawer where navigation links stagger in vertically (60ms delay per item) and the CTA button is pinned fixed to the screen bottom.

#### 5. Recreation for The Book Launch Company
* **Visual Treatment:** Floating pill using `#F6F1E8` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a crisp 1px `#ECE4D6` border (in dark mode: `#121115` at 85% opacity with 1px `#272727` border).
* **Links:** `Services` (with dropdown for 4 pillars), `Work`, `About`, `For Publishers`, and primary CTA `Book a call`.
* **CTA Button:** Solid `#FF5A36` Launch Coral button with white text and `rounded-full` curvature.

#### 6. What Changes for Our Business
* Replaces tech agency links with literary-specific anchors (`For Publishers & Agencies`, `Book Launches`).
* Includes a subtle literary wordmark ("The Book Launch Company") with an editorial serif character.

### Section 01: Hero Section

#### 1. Atomik Growth Original Composition
* Asymmetrical 2-column hero within a 1280px container.
* Left Column (~55%): Status pill/eyebrow -> High-impact H1 headline -> Supporting 1-line descriptor -> Twin CTA buttons (Primary Solid + Secondary Outline) -> Micro-proof badge.
* Right Column (~45%): Dynamic media cluster consisting of two or three staggered, overlapping vertical phone mockups showcasing real short-form video clips looping with view counts.

#### 2. Layout & Proportions
* Viewport height: ~90vh to 100vh on desktop (1440x900).
* Left column max width: ~640px.
* Gap between columns: 48px to 64px.
* Vertical alignment: Centered vertically within the viewport.

#### 3. Typography & Spacing
* H1 Headline: Massive geometric grotesk (`text-6xl` to `text-7xl` / 60px–72px), tight line-height (1.05 to 1.1), semibold (600–700). Max 3–4 lines with intentional breaks.
* Subhead: 18px–20px (`text-lg` to `text-xl`), regular/medium weight, muted contrast, line-height 1.5.
* CTA Group: 16px semibold buttons, 16px gap between primary and secondary buttons.
* Micro-proof line: 12px–14px muted text paired with mini star badges or avatar stacks.

#### 4. Media & Video Treatment
* iPhone-style bezel frames with curved corners (`rounded-[32px]`), subtle outer drop shadow, and 1px border.
* Videos: Looping vertical MP4s (9:16 aspect ratio), muted by default, with an overlay pill showing live view counts (`1.4M views`).
* Video tilt: Slight 3D rotational perspective (`rotate-[-3deg]` and `rotate-[4deg]`) creating depth.

#### 5. Interaction & Motion Behavior
* Page load entrance (`data-enter`): Text elements rise 24px and fade in smoothly over 600ms using a custom deceleration curve (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
* Phone frames glide into position with a subtle parallax float.
* Video autoplay: Starts immediately in viewport, pauses if scrolled out.

#### 6. Recreation for The Book Launch Company
* **Approved Headline:** `Launch the book. Build the author.`
* **Supporting Copy:** `Book launches, backlist revivals, author brands, and podcasts. Creative, social-first campaigns driven by the team behind @whynotnovels.`
* **CTAs:** Primary solid `#FF5A36` `Book a discovery call` + Secondary ghost `See our launches ↓`.
* **Micro-Proof:** `Trusted by authors across 12+ countries · 13M+ readers reached`.
* **Hero Visual:** Staggered dual-phone mockups running viral BookTok/Reel book hook videos, accompanied by a subtle floating 3D hardcover book render.

#### 7. What Changes for Our Business
* Replaces tech launch videos with BookTok hooks, trope reveals (#EnemiesToLovers, #DarkAcademia), and author interviews.
* Replaces tech font with an editorial serif headline (*Fraunces* or *Lora*) featuring italicized emphasis accents.

### Section 03: Metrics Band ("By the Numbers")

#### 1. Atomik Growth Original Composition
* Dedicated full-width dark section creating strong visual contrast against lighter surrounding areas.
* 3 to 4 massive data callouts aligned horizontally across a clean grid.
* Each metric consists of a giant number + unit, followed by a succinct 1-to-2 line label below.

#### 2. Layout & Proportions
* Desktop: 4-column balanced grid or 3-column wide layout.
* Section padding: 80px to 100px vertical.
* Content container: 1200px max width.

#### 3. Typography & Spacing
* Display Numbers: Huge display type (`text-6xl` to `text-8xl` / 64px–96px), tight letter-spacing, bold.
* Unit Symbols (`M+`, `K+`, `%`): Styled slightly smaller or superscripted.
* Labels: 14px–16px (`text-sm` to `text-base`), uppercase or clean title case, stone/muted color, letter-spacing `0.05em`.
* Gap between number and label: 12px to 16px.

#### 4. Interaction & Motion Behavior
* Scroll-triggered number count-up animation (`0 → 13M+`) executing once when 30% of the section enters the viewport.
* Numbers use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter during count-up.

#### 5. Recreation for The Book Launch Company
* **Visual Styling:** High-contrast Carbon Ink band (`#121115`) with warm Bone text (`#ECE4D6`).
* **Display Typography:** Monumental serif numbers (*Fraunces* display weight) conveying literary prestige.
* **Metric Matrix:**
  * **`13M+`** — *Readers reached across campaigns in 2025–2026*
  * **`50K+`** — *Dedicated reader community on Ariba's @whynotnovels*
  * **`1.4M`** — *Views delivered on our top single book campaign*
  * **`100%`** — *Literary focus: dedicated solely to authors and books*

#### 6. What Changes for Our Business
* Replaces B2B SaaS ARR and demo metrics with verified reader impressions, creator saves, and community reach.
* Labels strictly include timeframes and units, avoiding unlabelled vanity metrics.

* Single-column, centered editorial statement section.
* High typographic hierarchy: Large headline challenging conventional wisdom ("The Old Playbook Is Broken").
* Compact, rhythmic paragraph copy articulating market friction and introducing the new-media alternative.

#### 2. Layout & Proportions
* Max-width: 720px to 800px centered.
* Generous negative space: 96px to 128px vertical padding.

#### 3. Typography & Spacing

* Body: 18px–20px (`text-lg` to `text-xl`), line-height 1.6, balanced text wrapping (`text-wrap: balance`).
* Accent text: Selective color emphasis on 2–3 key words.

#### 4. Interaction & Motion Behavior
* Scroll reveal: Words or lines reveal progressively with an opacity transition as the reader scrolls.

#### 5. Recreation for The Book Launch Company
* **Headline:** `Great books don't sell themselves anymore.`
* **Body Copy:** `Readers no longer browse quiet bookstore aisles to find their next read. They discover it on their phones—in a 7-second Reel, an author's stack, or a creator recommendation. A book release without short-form content is just a whisper. We turn your launch into an event readers talk about.`
* **Color Accents:** Launch Coral (`#FF5A36`) highlights on words `whisper` and `event`.

#### 6. What Changes for Our Business
* Directly targets the emotional and commercial reality of author frustration with traditional PR silence.

### Section 05: The Four Service Pillars

#### 1. Atomik Growth Original Composition
* 3 to 4 large Bento-style service cards arranged in a 2x2 grid or asymmetric 3-card layout.
* Each card acts as a "mini product UI":
  * Card Header: Numbered badge (`01`, `02`), service name, and 1-line benefit promise.
  * Card Media: An active smartphone or browser UI mockup playing real short-form video or displaying live distribution progress meters.
  * Deliverable Checklist: 3 clear checkmark items of tangible inclusions.
  * Card Link: "Explore [Service] →".
* Zero content hidden behind hover states.

#### 2. Layout & Proportions
* Desktop: 2-column × 2-row grid with 32px gap.
* Card height: ~480px to 540px.
* Corner radius: `rounded-2xl` (16px to 24px) with subtle 1px border.

#### 3. Typography & Spacing
* Service Title: 24px–28px (`text-2xl`), semibold.
* Promise Line: 16px medium.
* Bullets: 14px regular with custom accent icons.

#### 4. Interaction & Motion Behavior
* Card hover: Subtle 2px–3px vertical lift with shadow deepening (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
* Embedded phone video loops continuously without audio.

#### 5. Recreation for The Book Launch Company
* **Four Cards:**
  1. `01 · Book Launches` (Pre-launch, Launch week blitz, Post-launch tour)
  2. `02 · Backlist Revival` (Trope re-targeting, comp-title pairing, creator waves)

#### 6. What Changes for Our Business
