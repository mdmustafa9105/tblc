## 1. Executive Summary & Creative Reconstruction

This document serves as the master client handover report for the complete reference-led experience reconstruction of **The Book Launch Company** website.

Rather than settling for a generic marketing agency layout, the website has been rebuilt from top to bottom following an in-depth empirical audit of [Atomik Growth](https://www.atomikgrowth.com/) (documented in [`REFERENCE-EXPERIENCE-ANALYSIS.md`](file:///d:/TheBookLaunchCompany/REFERENCE-EXPERIENCE-ANALYSIS.md)). The result is an original, literary-editorial experience that mirrors Atomik Growth's structural confidence, storytelling momentum, living deliverable widgets, and conversion journey while expressing The Book Launch Company's distinct brand identity: *"Editorial ink and paper, with a spark of launch energy"*.

### Core Structural Transformations:
1. **Hero Composition (55/45 Split):** Category-defining headline (*"The New-Media Launch Partner for Authors"*), monumental display typography (*"Launch the book. Build the author."*), dual high-intent CTAs (*"Discuss Your Book"* and *"Explore Our Work"*), paired with a 9:16 vertical campaign video deliverable with audio toggle and real-time BookTok metrics (`1.4M views`).
2. **Core Thesis & Services Bento:** Direct translation of Atomik Growth's *"Distribution is the moat. We build yours."* into our literary counterpart:
   * **Headline:** *"Reader attention is the moat. We build yours."*
   * **4 Living Deliverable Widgets:**
     * *Launch Virality:* Interactive launch timeline slider (-8wks → Launch Day → +4wks) with deliverable breakdowns and expected traction.
     * *Backlist Revival:* Interactive Trope Repositioning Simulator (`#EnemiesToLovers`, `#DarkAcademia`, `#MorallyGrey`) showing algorithmic audience reach and comp title alignment.
     * *Author Brand & Content:* Living Weekly Content Sprint Queue showing real-time production status (`Edited & Ready`, `In Edit Suite`, `Script Approved`) and honest `~1 hr/wk` filming requirement.
     * *Author Podcasts:* The Repurposing Waterfall diagram demonstrating 1 recorded session yielding 15+ multi-channel assets.
3. **"Our Work and Reach, by the Numbers":** High-contrast ink band presenting verified proof metrics (`13M+` Reach, `50K+` Community, `1.4M` Top Campaign, `100%` Literary Focus).
4. **Deep Editorial Case Studies:** Replaces generic portfolio cards with full-width narrative campaign features directly matching Atomik Growth's Acquired (52M) and Helena (3.6M) structures. Includes challenge narratives, creative hook breakdowns, 4-metric KPI grids, and transparent `[TBC Concept Prototype]` badges.
5. **Security-Fortified API & Honest Booking:** Server-side rate limiting, honeypot bot trap, script sanitization, masked PII logging, and local lead preservation in `data/inquiries.jsonl`.

## 5. Security & Verification Summary

1. **Security Testing (`scripts/test-api-security.cjs`):**
   * `GET /api/inquiry`: **405 Method Not Allowed** (Guarded against unauthorized scraping)
   * Empty / Malformed Payload: **400 Bad Request**
   * Invalid Email Syntax: **400 Bad Request**
   * Honeypot Bot Trap (`hp_website`): **Silently Trapped (200)** without alert noise
   * Sanitized Output: `<script>` tags cleanly stripped; client IP masked; logged to `data/inquiries.jsonl`.
2. **Responsive Layouts (5 Viewports):**
   * `1440px Desktop`: `scrollWidth: 1432px` | `innerWidth: 1440px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `1280px Laptop`: `scrollWidth: 1272px` | `innerWidth: 1280px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `768px Tablet`: `scrollWidth: 760px` | `innerWidth: 768px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `390px Mobile`: `scrollWidth: 390px` | `innerWidth: 390px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `360px Small Mobile`: `scrollWidth: 360px` | `innerWidth: 360px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
3. **Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA Compliance):**
   * Exactly 1 `<h1>` tag; strictly hierarchical `<h2>`–`<h4>` structure.
   * `scroll-padding-top: 88px` prevents sticky header occlusion on focus.
   * Interactive touch targets meet or exceed **44×44px / 48×48px**.
   * Accessible Marquee Pause/Resume toggle with ARIA announcements.
