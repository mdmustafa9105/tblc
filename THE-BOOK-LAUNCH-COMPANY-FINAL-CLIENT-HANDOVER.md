## 1. Executive Summary & Creative Reconstruction

This document serves as the master client handover report for the complete reference-led experience reconstruction of **The Book Launch Company** website.

To resolve client feedback regarding over-complication, the website was comprehensively streamlined to mirror the exact, confident, minimalist architecture of [Atomik Growth](https://www.atomikgrowth.com/). Redundant cards, competing sub-sections, and clutter were eliminated in favor of a crisp, direct 9-stage conversion rhythm.

The result is a unified, editorial experience that mirrors Atomik Growth's structural confidence, storytelling momentum, living deliverable widgets, and conversion journey while expressing The Book Launch Company's distinct brand identity: *"Editorial ink and paper, with a spark of launch energy"*.

### Core Structural Transformations:
1. **Hero Composition (55/45 Split):** Category-defining kicker (*"The New-Media Launch Partner for Authors"*), monumental display typography (*"Launch the book. Build the author."*), dual high-intent CTAs (*"Discuss Your Book"* and *"See Our Work ↓"*), paired with ONE clean, focused 9:16 vertical video player with sound toggle, live view pill (`1.4M views`), and real-time BookTok metrics.
2. **Early Credibility Strip:** Continuous, smooth infinite book cover marquee (*"We partner with authors and publishers worldwide"*), with accessible pause/resume control.
3. **Core Thesis & Services Bento:** Direct translation of Atomik Growth's *"Distribution is the moat. We build yours."* into our literary counterpart:
   * **Headline:** *"Reader attention is the moat. We build yours."*
   * **4 Living Deliverable Widgets:**
     * *Launch Virality:* Interactive launch timeline selector (Pre-Launch → Launch Blitz → Read-Through) with deliverable breakdowns and expected traction.
     * *Backlist Revival:* Interactive Trope Repositioning Simulator (`#EnemiesToLovers`, `#DarkAcademia`, `#MorallyGreyHero`) showing algorithmic audience reach and comp title alignment.
     * *Author Brand & Content:* Living Weekly Content Sprint Queue showing real-time production status (`Ready to Post`, `In Edit Suite`, `Script Approved`) and honest `~1 hr/wk` filming requirement.
     * *Author Podcasts:* The Repurposing Waterfall diagram demonstrating 1 recorded session yielding 15+ multi-channel assets.
4. **"Our Work and Reach, by the Numbers":** High-contrast dark ink breakout band presenting verified proof metrics (`13M+` Reach, `50K+` Community, `1.4M` Top Campaign, `100%` Literary Focus).
5. **Deep Editorial Case Studies:** Full-width narrative campaign features directly matching Atomik Growth's Acquired (52M) and Helena (3.6M) structures. Includes challenge narratives, creative hook breakdowns, 4-metric KPI grids, book mockup canvases, and transparent `[TBC Concept Prototype]` badges.
6. **In-House Media Engine:** Demonstrates founder Ariba's proprietary reader community (@whynotnovels, 50K+ readers) as an active distribution moat.
7. **High-Intent Objections FAQ:** Full-width, generous accordion directly addressing the 5 core author objections (turnaround, ~1 hr/wk time, self-published vs. traditional, sales guarantees, platforms).
8. **High-Contrast Closing Conversion Band ("Work With Us"):** Direct, confident call to action with 1-on-1 strategy assurance.
9. **Minimal 4-Column Footer:** Clean editorial footer matching Atomik's column structure.
10. **Security-Fortified API & Honest Booking:** Server-side rate limiting, honeypot bot trap, script sanitization, masked PII logging, and local lead preservation in `data/inquiries.jsonl`.

## 5. Security & Verification Summary

1. **Security Testing (`scripts/test-api-security.cjs`):**
   * `GET /api/inquiry`: **405 Method Not Allowed** (Guarded against unauthorized scraping)
   * Empty / Malformed Payload: **400 Bad Request**
   * Invalid Email Syntax: **400 Bad Request**
   * Honeypot Bot Trap (`hp_website`): **Silently Trapped (200)** without alert noise
   * Sanitized Output: `<script>` tags cleanly stripped; client IP masked; logged to `data/inquiries.jsonl`.
2. **Responsive Layouts (5 Viewports):**
   * `1440px Desktop`: `scrollWidth: 1432px` | `innerWidth: 1440px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `1280px Laptop`: `scrollWidth: 1272px` | `innerWidth: 1280px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `768px Tablet`: `scrollWidth: 760px` | `innerWidth: 768px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `390px Mobile`: `scrollWidth: 390px` | `innerWidth: 390px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
   * `360px Small Mobile`: `scrollWidth: 360px` | `innerWidth: 360px` (0px overflow) — **[PASS]**
3. **Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA Compliance):**
   * Exactly 1 `<h1>` tag; strictly hierarchical `<h2>`–`<h4>` structure.
   * `scroll-padding-top: 88px` prevents sticky header occlusion on focus.
   * Interactive touch targets meet or exceed **44×44px / 48×48px**.
   * Accessible Marquee Pause/Resume toggle with ARIA announcements.
