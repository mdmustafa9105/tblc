## Verification Protocol
Every published statistic must conform to the 3-point agency rule:
1. **Unit Label**: Every number must state its unit (views, accounts reached, saves, shares).
2. **Timeframe**: Every metric must state its measurement window (e.g. "over 30 days post-launch").
3. **Audit Trail**: Source platform analytics screenshot or export held on file.
